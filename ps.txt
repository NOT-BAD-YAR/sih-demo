This problem is stronger for your team than a
normal civic-reporting app because its central
technology is not merely a webpage or API
integration. You must build a
behavioral-analysis engine that learns normal
activity and identifies suspicious deviations.
Modern UEBA systems analyze users, devices,
servers, applications, and network activity
using behavioral baselines and machine
learning

What Problem Does It Solve?

Traditional security tools usually detect known
threats using signatures or fixed rules. They
may miss a legitimate employee whose account
has been compromised or an insider who
misuses valid access.

For example:
· An employee normally downloads 20 MB per
day but suddenly downloads 5 GB.
. A user logs in from two distant locations
within a short time.
· An account accesses files outside its usual
department.
. A dormant account becomes active at an
unusual hour.
· A server suddenly communicates with a
device it has never contacted before.

The key challenge is that the action may look
legitimate by itself. The danger becomes visible
only when the system understands the user's
normal behavior and the context around the
event
